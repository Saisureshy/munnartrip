All timings are planning estimates. Christmas traffic, road conditions, weather, restaurant queues and hill-road delays can change the schedule. Use live Google Maps navigation on the travel day.

Revised return plan: Alleppey houseboat on Dec 28, Marari Beach early on Dec 29, then drive via Tenkasi–Madurai–Salem to Bengaluru and overnight there. Bengaluru to Hyderabad on Dec 30 is a long drive; the old 12:30 PM arrival target is not realistic. Plan for evening arrival and never rush or drive without proper rest.

Day 3 · Saturday, December 26

Coimbatore → Pollachi → Aliyar → Udumalpet → Chinnar → Marayoor → Munnar

This is our scenic drive day. The Coimbatore–Munnar route through Pollachi, Udumalpet, Chinnar and Marayoor is approximately 160–170 km before sightseeing detours. Allow a full day with stops.

Route rule: Fuel and have a proper meal in Pollachi or Udumalpet. Forest and hill sections have fewer convenient services. Cross the Chinnar/Marayoor stretch in daylight.
