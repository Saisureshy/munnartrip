Open Hyderabad to Munnar Route

Bengaluru and Coimbatore are only route halts and overnight stays. Munnar is our only sightseeing destination.

Important safety note: Munnar to Bengaluru is a long drive, and Bengaluru to Hyderabad is another long journey. Reaching Hyderabad by December 30 noon after proper sleep may not be realistic. Please don't compromise sleep or drive while tired. Evening arrival or changing the return schedule is safer.
