Trip style: Road trip with Bengaluru and Coimbatore as route halts. Munnar is our only holiday destination. Ooty and Wayanad are excluded.

All times are planning estimates. Christmas traffic, weather, road conditions, queues and restaurant timings can change the schedule. Use live Google Maps navigation on the travel day.

Important return timing: Munnar to Bengaluru is a long drive, and Bengaluru to Hyderabad is another long drive. Reaching Hyderabad by Dec 30 at 12:30 PM after the Dec 29 drive is not a safe, realistic promise. Please adjust the return time or trip dates. Never drive sleepy, speed, or skip rest to meet a deadline.
